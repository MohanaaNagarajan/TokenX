1. App Owner / Developer

TokenX ("the App") is developed and managed by the KRCT Hostel Administration team. We act as the data controller for the information collected through the application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use TokenX, we collect the following types of data:

  • Personal Information: Name, college email address, department, year of study, and hostel room number.
  • Usage Information: Generated food tokens, food preferences (Vegetarian/Non-Vegetarian), token application dates, complaints, and feedback.
  • Device Data: Basic device identifiers required for secure application operation and QR code functionalities.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the hostel mess services. This includes generating QR-based food tokens, verifying student identity, calculating daily food demand, handling food-related complaints, and allowing the hostel warden to monitor mess operations effectively.

4. Customer Data

In the context of TokenX, "Customer Data" refers to the student data. This data is kept strictly confidential. Students can only view their own tokens, complaints, and feedback. They cannot view the personal data or tokens of other students.

5. Professional Data

Administrative and professional data collected by the hostel warden (such as overall token counts, aggregate complaint analytics, and internal notes regarding food quality) are restricted entirely to authorized warden accounts and are not visible to the general student body.

6. Communication

We use your registered college email address solely for authentication purposes and critical mess-related administrative communications. We do not use your email for marketing or promotional purposes.

7. Third-Party Services

TokenX uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication) as its backend service provider. Firebase securely processes and stores your authentication and app data in compliance with standard security protocols.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared internally with the authorized Hostel Administration (Warden) to facilitate token verification and complaint resolution. Data may only be disclosed externally if required by law.

9. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including Firebase Security Rules, to ensure that only authorized users can access specific records. Strict role-based access control guarantees that student data remains private from other users.

10. Data Retention

We retain your personal information, token history, and complaints for as long as you are actively enrolled in the hostel system or as necessary to fulfill administrative auditing and reporting requirements.

11. Account Deletion

If you leave the hostel or wish to have your account deleted, please submit a direct request to the Hostel Warden. Upon verification, your account credentials and associated personal token history will be removed from the active database.

12. Children's Privacy

TokenX is intended solely for enrolled college students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such data, we will delete it immediately.

13. Location Data

TokenX does not collect, track, or store your GPS or device location data at any time.

14. Notifications

The app may display in-app notifications regarding your token status (Applied, Confirmed, Used) or complaint status updates (Under Review, Resolved). Push notifications, if enabled, will only be used for critical mess updates.

15. Cookies / Tracking

TokenX does not use traditional web cookies. However, the app utilizes local device storage (such as SharedPreferences) strictly to maintain your active login session and cache temporary token states for offline reliability.

16. User Rights

You have the right to access the personal data stored in your profile, view your complete token and complaint history directly within the app, and request corrections to any inaccurate information by contacting the administration.

17. Changes to Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be updated within the app and the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy will reflect the date of the latest revision.
